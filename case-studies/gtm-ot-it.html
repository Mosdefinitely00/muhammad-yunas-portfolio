Translate Plant Economics for the CFO

A positioning and enablement playbook that retires “IIoT platform” jargon and reframes predictive telemetry as risk reduction and EBITDA preservation — so OT sponsors and IT champions stop vetoing each other.

The Challenge

Industrial buyers reject platform jargon. Plant managers care about uptime and safety; CFOs care about risk and EBITDA. Product, sales, and delivery often run disconnected messaging — so pipeline looks large while conversion stalls at the OT/IT handoff.

Without a shared category language, every deal re-educates the market from scratch. Velocity dies in discovery.

The Approach

1. Retire commoditized language. Replace “IIoT platform” with Physical Operations Intelligence — a frame that ties telemetry to plant economics.

2. Map the buying committee. Every account gets an OT Sponsor, IT Champion, and CFO Economic Buyer. Messaging and enablement are role-based, not one-deck-fits-all.

3. Structure the motion. Open-ended trials become 60-day POCs tied to downtime and safety benchmarks. Sales is treated as the primary customer of the narrative.

Verified Outcomes

Stronger executive sponsorship, clearer pipeline quality, and shorter decision cycles when the narrative is tied to plant economics — the same motion that supported 40% cycle compression and precision industrial segmentation in related OT/IT engagements.
