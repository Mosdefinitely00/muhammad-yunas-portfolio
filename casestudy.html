The GTM Transformation: Re-architecting the Category Narrative.

The Challenge

The Feature Trap & The OT/IT Divide.

A recurring theme across my career is inheriting brilliant technology trapped behind weak positioning. The products are sold on feature comparisons, the sales cycles are agonizingly long, and OT buyers (Plant Managers) and IT buyers (CIOs) veto each other because the vendor fails to speak both languages. My playbook breaks this cycle.

01
Listen Deeply (Audit)Narrow the ICP. Identify the specific friction points between the OT Sponsor and the IT Champion. Stop selling to "everyone".
02
Clarify Relentlessly (Narrative)Abandon the "Feature Trap." Translate complex API and compliance capabilities into CFO-aligned business cases.
03
Redesign the Motion (Enablement)Treat sales as the primary customer. Build dynamic battle cards and shift from subjective slide decks to structured POCs.
