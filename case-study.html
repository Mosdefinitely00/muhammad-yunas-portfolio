Case Study: OT/IT Convergence GTM

How a broken GTM engine was rebuilt into a $40M ARR industrial AI business.

THE CHALLENGE

Strong engineering, zero commercial traction. ICP undefined, sales lacked OT fluency, no category narrative, no partner ecosystem, quota attainment below 60%.

THE GTM REBUILD

01 — ICP Redefined

Focused on discrete manufacturing and upstream energy. Identified OT Sponsor + IT Champion + Economic Buyer.

02 — Category Narrative Built

Reframed from “IIoT platform” to “Physical AI Operations.” Anchored messaging in operational autonomy.

03 — Sales Motion Redesigned

OT Discovery → Digital Twin POC → Full Deployment. POCs tied to downtime, throughput, or energy savings.

04 — Partner Ecosystem Constructed

Prioritized SIs with OT DNA. Integrated with major PLC/SCADA vendors. Established AWS/Azure co-sell.

05 — Cyber-Physical Layer Added

Elevated OT security to board-level governance. Increased average deal size 40%.

THE RESULTS
$40M+ ARR in 30 months
137% Peak quota attainment
14→6 Month cycle reduction
35% Partner-sourced ARR
