Case Study: OT/IT Convergence GTM

How a broken GTM engine was rebuilt into a $40M ARR industrial AI business.

THE CHALLENGE

The platform had strong engineering depth but zero commercial traction. ICP undefined, sales team lacked OT fluency, no category narrative, and partner ecosystem nonexistent. Quota attainment below 60%.

THE GTM REBUILD

01 — ICP Redefined

Focused on discrete manufacturing and upstream energy. Identified OT Sponsor + IT Champion + Economic Buyer for every deal.

02 — Category Narrative Built

Reframed from “IIoT platform” to “Physical AI Operations.” Anchored messaging in operational autonomy and real-time decisioning.

03 — Sales Motion Redesigned

Three-stage motion: OT Discovery → Digital Twin POC → Full Deployment. POCs tied to downtime, throughput, or energy savings.

04 — Partner Ecosystem Constructed

Prioritized SIs with OT DNA. Integrated with major PLC/SCADA vendors. Established AWS/Azure co-sell programs.

05 — Cyber-Physical Layer Added

Elevated OT security to board-level governance. Increased average deal size 40%.

THE RESULTS
$40M+ ARR in 30 months
137% Peak quota attainment
14→6 Month cycle reduction
35% Partner-sourced ARR
