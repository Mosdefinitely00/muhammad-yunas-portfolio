Bridging Deep Tech and Frictionless Commerce

I don't just market products; I understand the engineering constraints of the physical world. A defining example of this was translating a highly complex hardware innovation into a clear supply chain narrative.

We engineered a Bluetooth tracking sticker that consumed zero external power—it harvested energy entirely from ambient facility lighting (the lights in the ceiling and natural sun). Rather than marketing the complex thermodynamics of energy harvesting, I positioned it around frictionless commerce.

By placing these passive nodes on SKUs, we achieved absolute visibility: knowing exactly when a product left the factory floor, how it moved through the warehouse, and precisely where it landed on retail shelves.
