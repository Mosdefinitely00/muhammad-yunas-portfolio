Physical AI Glossary

A reference guide for the core concepts behind Physical AI and Industrial Autonomy.

Digital Twin
A governed model of physical assets, processes, and behaviors.
Physical AI
AI systems that interpret physical signals and make operational decisions.
Edge Inference
Sub‑10ms decision cycles executed at the point of operation.
Autonomy Orchestration
Coordinated multi‑agent decision‑making across machines, lines, and facilities.
OT/IT Convergence
The collapse of operational and information systems into a unified architecture.
Agentic AI
AI systems that perceive, plan, act, and self‑correct autonomously.
