# Featured section — visual layout

Mirrors a LinkedIn Featured row, built as a site section. Use `case-study-card` rhythm and the thumbnail system.

## Grid
Desktop: 4 columns, 16px gap, two rows. Tablet: 2 columns. Mobile: 1 column, horizontal snap optional, 16px gap. Section max-width 1120px, centered. Align card tops. Equal card height.

## Card
Thumbnail 16:9 on top. Title, Inter Semibold 18px white. Description, 14–15px muted, two lines max, then ellipsis. CTA is a ghost button, bottom-left of the card: “Open”. Gold on hover, border to gold. No underline.

1. Website — live portfolio. CTA to home.
2. Physical AI GTM Playbook — $0 to $40M motion. CTA to the OT/IT case.
3. Industrial Autonomy GTM Framework — four layers. CTA to /physical-ai/.
4. Silicon Meets Steel — manifesto. CTA to the essay.
5. Physical AI Cockpit — industry, constraint, outcome. CTA to cockpit.
6. DCIM Case Study — infrastructure intelligence. CTA to the case.
7. Agentic AI Commercialization — partner and seller motion. CTA to the brief.
8. Mastering a Product Launch — launch system. CTA to the brief.

## Spacing
Section label, then H2 “Featured”, then 24px, then grid. 32px below the grid before the next section. Thumbnails use the same navy, blue, gold, silver system. Hover does not move layout.
