# Homepage hero graphic — handoff for the animation engine

Do not replace the current hero type. This is the graphic that sits with it, matching /physical-ai/.

## Geometry
Fixed frame, 16:9 desktop, 4:5 mobile. Navy field. Four horizontal bands, equal height, inset 8%.
1. Digital Twin — faint blue grid, asset outlines in steel.
2. Physical AI — five nodes, one gold decision node, thin blue links.
3. Edge inference — two gold lightning strokes, short, no glow bloom.
4. Autonomy orchestration — six nodes on one line, connected, system at rest.

## Lighting
No gradients that wash the navy. Blue at 35–80% opacity. Gold only on the active decision and the final lock line. White type only for the lock-in.

## Motion
One pass, about 7 seconds, then hold. Order: twin grid fades in, asset outlines draw, nodes light, inference strokes flash under 200ms, orchestration links connect, lock-in reads “Industrial Autonomy Infrastructure — Online”. Replay on click. No camera move. No orbit.

## Desktop vs mobile
Desktop: bands span the graphic, labels at the left of each band. Mobile: same four bands, labels above each band, nodes reduced to three, lock-in wraps to two lines. No horizontal scroll.
