# Thumbnail concepts — eight executive briefs

Shared system. 1200×675. Dark navy field (#0A0F1F). No photography. No people. One geometric motif per brief, drawn in electric blue (#1A7CFF) at low opacity, with a single gold (#D4A857) accent. Title in white Inter Semibold, lower left, two lines max. Steel silver (#C8CCD4) hairline frame, 24px inset. Small gold rule under the title. Category label in steel, 11px, tracked uppercase, top left: PHYSICAL AI.

1. Silicon Meets Steel
A faint twin grid across the field. Left half is a steel outline of a plant bay. Right half is the same bay as nodes. One gold beam crosses the center where the two meet. Title sits on the beam.

2. Mastering a Product Launch
Five horizontal bars, increasing in length, like a launch ramp. The last bar is gold. A single blue node sits at the end of the ramp. No arrows, no rockets.

3. GTM Architecture — Sense → Reason → Act
Three stacked chevrons, open, left to right. Sense is a silver grid. Reason is blue nodes. Act is a gold stroke. The title uses the arrow words as the second line.

4. OT/IT Convergence
Two rectangles, plant and enterprise, overlapping by a third. The overlap is a blue lattice. A gold packet sits in the overlap. Title under the lattice.

5. Agentic AI Commercialization
Four small nodes in a shallow arc, linked by thin blue lines. One node is gold and slightly larger — the commercial decision. Background grid is almost invisible.

6. Physical AI Cockpit
A rounded instrument frame. Inside, three short readouts as horizontal rules (silver, blue, gold). No fake numbers. Title centered under the frame.

7. Industrial Autonomy GTM Framework
Four thin horizontal layers, labeled only by weight of line: twin, physical AI, edge, orchestration. Orchestration is gold. The others are blue on navy. Title to the left of the stack.

8. Executive GTM Brief
Almost empty. One gold horizontal rule, one blue node on the rule, wide navy field. Title large. This thumbnail should feel like letterhead, not a diagram.
